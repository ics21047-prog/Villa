Welcome

Right on the water's edge

Perched directly above the beach in the quiet seaside village of Kokkino Nero, Veranda sto Aigaio is a spacious villa built for slow mornings and golden sunsets. Step out of the living room onto a wide stone terrace, settle in beneath the umbrella, and let the sound of the waves do the rest.

Warm terracotta tones, a cosy fireplace, a fully equipped kitchen and bright sea-view bedrooms make it an ideal retreat for families and groups who want the beach quite literally on their doorstep.

Location

Kokkino Nero, Aegean coast

Tucked along the eastern shore of Thessaly beneath the slopes of Mount Kissavos (Ossa), Kokkino Nero — "red water" — is a tranquil seaside village famous for its natural mineral springs that flow straight to the sea. It's a place of pine-shaded coves, long pebbly beaches and authentic Greek tavernas.

  • On the beach — sea & sand at your doorstep
  • ~2 min walk — village tavernas & café
  • ~5 min — famous Kokkino Nero mineral springs
  • ~45 min — Larissa city
  • ~1 hr — Volos & Mount Pelion
